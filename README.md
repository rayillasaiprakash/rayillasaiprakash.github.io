## About

Early-career researcher from Telangana, India  
**M.A. Linguistics** | **M.Sc. Geo-Informatics** (Osmania University)

Research focus:
- Language variation & dialectology (Telangana Telugu)
- Language documentation & oral narratives
- Sociolinguistics, multilingualism & heritage languages
- Interdisciplinary work linking linguistics with Geo-Informatics and sustainability (Siri Dhanya / millets)

- **Telangana Proverbs Corpus** — 40 entries with speaker explanations, translations, themes and linguistic notes
- **Jagtial Festival Narratives** — Oral documentation of six local festivals
- **TT–ST Lexicon** — Growing comparative lexicon of Telangana Telugu vs Standard Telugu
- **Siri Dhanya Research** — Ongoing work on millet cultivation, water productivity and GIS suitability
