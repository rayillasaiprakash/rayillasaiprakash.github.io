Research Projects

Completed Thesis Research

M.A. Linguistics Thesis

Exploring Regional Linguistic Variation in Telangana Telugu: The Karimnagar District in Focus

Study of regional linguistic variation in the Karimnagar variety of Telangana Telugu, contributing to dialect documentation and sociolinguistic research. Based on fieldwork with local speakers.

M.Sc. Geo-Informatics Thesis

Urbanisation Impact on Groundwater Resources: A Geospatial Analysis

Examined the spatial impact of urban expansion on groundwater resources using GIS, remote sensing and spatial analysis methods.

Independent Language Documentation Work

These projects were developed independently after the MA thesis and form a coherent research programme on Telangana Telugu.

Telangana Language Documentation Programme

  • Telangana Voices — Digital Language & Culture Archive
  • Telangana Proverbs Corpus — 40 proverbs with speaker explanations, English translations, themes, semantic domains and linguistic notes
  • TT–ST Lexicon — 173-entry comparative lexicon (Telangana Telugu ↔ Standard Telugu + English glosses)
  • Jagtial Festival Narratives — Oral documentation of six local festivals (Hair-Offering, Jatara, Ugadi, Shivarathri, Sankranti/Katrevula Panduga, Dasara) with phonological, morphological and semantic analysis
