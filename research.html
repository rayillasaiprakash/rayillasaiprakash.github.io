Research

My research is centred on Telangana Telugu. I document regional language use and cultural practices and use linguistic and geospatial methods to study variation, identity, and community knowledge.

Why This Research Matters

Telangana Telugu contains diverse regional varieties and forms of oral knowledge that deserve systematic documentation. My aim is to create researchable digital resources while studying the social meanings attached to regional speech and the ways linguistic practices change across communities and generations.

Current Documentation Work

  • Karimnagar Dialect Study — MA research on regional linguistic variation.
  • TT–ST Lexicon — 173-entry comparative lexical resource.
  • Telangana Proverbs Corpus — 40 documented proverb entries with explanations, translations and linguistic notes.
  • Telangana Festival Corpus — fieldwork-based narratives from Jagtial district, with speaker information and linguistic/cultural analysis.
  • Telangana Voices — digital documentation of spoken language and culture.

Interdisciplinary Work

My M.Sc. training in Geo-Informatics also supports an independent line of environmental research. My current Siri Dhanya study documents community-reported millet cultivation and water-use practices in the Jagtial–Karimnagar region, compares them with published water-footprint evidence, and proposes a future GIS suitability analysis.

Independent Interdisciplinary Research

Siri Dhanya Millet Cultivation and Water Sustainability in Telangana — manuscript in development. The project combines field documentation, secondary literature and a proposed GIS framework. The current evidence distinguishes community-reported estimates from published measurements and identifies controlled field validation as a next step.
