About Me

I am Rayilla Sai Prakash, an early-career researcher from Hyderabad, Telangana, India, with an interdisciplinary background in Linguistics and Geo-Informatics.

My primary research interests include sociolinguistics, language variation and change, multilingualism, language contact, heritage language maintenance, dialectology, and language documentation. I am particularly interested in documenting the linguistic diversity of Telangana and understanding how languages evolve across communities and generations.

Alongside linguistics, I work with GIS, spatial analysis and environmental research. This interdisciplinary orientation allows me to connect language documentation with digital technologies and geospatial methods.
